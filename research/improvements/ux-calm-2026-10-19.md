## 2. Header: at most ONE secondary row

The rule (MealBar.tsx) for a single food: the search and "+ Add" share row 1, then exactly one of these:
1. the text named several foods ("tea") → **"Which tea?"**, highlighted, in one line (scrolls sideways; "More ▾" at the end);
2. else, several drinks with no pace picked → **"Over how long?"** in one line (highlighted);
3. else, "Which …?" not answered yet → that row, plain, with "More ▾";
4. else → a pill **"Pizza · Capricciosa ▾"** / **"Beer · Light · over 2 h ▾"** that opens a sheet with the kinds, sub-kinds, add-ins, "What's on it?" and **Over how long?**

"Answered" means a pick, or the sheet was opened and closed. A new search asks again. A link that already names the kind (`v=`, `on=`, `add=`) opens with the pill. For a **meal**, its chips are the one row, and the pace is the first chip ("◷ Over how long? ▾" highlighted until picked, then "◷ Over 2 h ▾"), which opens a small sheet. The kcal caption moved into the sheet footer. The same layout is used on desktop.
