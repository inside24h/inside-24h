# Inside 24h - competitor and similar product research (2026-10-15)

Note: web search returned many secondary/affiliate sources; figures are rough, verify on vendor pages. No Reddit threads were retrievable directly.

## 1. Viral "what happens after X" content
- Renegade Pharmacist "1 hour after a can of Coke" infographic (2015, 0/10/20/40/45/60 min). Hugely shared, but non-peer-reviewed, heroin/dopamine comparisons criticised as rhetorical. 
  - https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/coca-cola-bodily-effects-one-hour
  - https://www.wtkr.com/2015/07/31/infographic-claims-to-show-what-happens-in-first-hour-after-you-drink-coca-cola
  - https://www.hcplive.com/view/one-coca-cola-one-hour-harmful-effects-on-the-body
  - https://www.fox29.com/news/what-happens-to-your-body-when-you-drink-a-can-of-coke
- Does well: simple minute timeline, shareable, curiosity hook. Weak: static, one product, fear-based, no evidence levels, only 1 hour.
- Inside 24h can do better: 24 h, 149 foods, evidence A/B/C/? (credibility vs. clickbait), shareable Reel export. Make "Coke myth vs evidence" a ready-made content series.

## 2. Closest functional matches (small)
- Duke "DiVE into Alcohol" (2008, alcohol only, 3D body journey) https://scholars.duke.edu/artistic-work/10000/work
- Strong Spirit Strong Mind Interactive Body Tool (AU, alcohol, click-body-parts) https://positivechoices.org.au/students/strong-spirit-strong-mind-interactive-body-tool/pdf/
- Human Digestive System iPad app ($0.99, food digestion animations, no drinks) https://apps.apple.com/app/id983535092
- Purify (caffeine/nicotine/alcohol remaining, donuts/countdowns) https://apps.apple.com/us/app/purify-%E5%92%96%E5%95%A1%E5%9B%A0-%E5%B0%BC%E5%8F%A4%E4%B8%81%E5%92%8C%E9%85%92%E7%B2%BE%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%86/id6744034199
- CaffPulse (caffeine as liquid container, scrub-forward decay chart) https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758062092
- Finding: no product combines real 3D anatomy + any food/drink + 24 h timeline + blood/organ effects. This is the core gap.

## 3. Glucose / nutrition apps
- Levels: now cheaper app membership (~$200/yr reported) + optional sensors (Stelo ~$100/mo); older model $398 upfront + $199/mo. Reviews (~2.9/5 on one site): expensive, complicated app, hard cancellation, sensor accuracy doubts, bot support. https://bodysciencereview.com/blog/levels-cgm-continuous-glucose-monitor-review/ https://thingtesting.com/brands/levels/reviews https://www.levels.com/pro/guides/cgm-program-pricing
- Zoe: starter kit ~$360 + $59-79/mo (US), UK ~GBP 25-60/mo; praised for insight, criticised for support, bugs, cancellation, data loss, "pricey placebo". https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-with-zoe-app-review-analysis-app-store-us-141301 https://www.startmotionmedia.com/2025/07/16/zoe-review-2024-data-driven-diet-or-pricey-placebo/
- Lumen: device $249-349 + subscription required; wants more concrete food plans. https://the5krunner.com/2020/12/13/lumen-review-metabolism-review/
- (Nutrisense: no data retrieved.)
- Lesson: all need hardware/personal data and cost 200-900/yr; users hate hard cancellation and paywalls. Inside 24h gives population-level "typical" glucose curves for free, no hardware. Copy: glycemic-response framing, "what to do next" concrete actions. Disclaimer: not personalised.

## 4. Calorie / scanner apps
- MyFitnessPal: loudest complaint is features moved behind paywall (barcode scan since 2022). Yuka: praise for easy ingredient rating; complaints subscription, privacy, scan issues, harsh "poor" ratings. Foodvisor: premium paywall, photo ID errors on mixed dishes. https://unstar.app/blog/calorie-tracking-apps-ranked-1-star-reviews-2026 https://kimola.com/reports/in-depth-yuka-app-customer-feedback-analysis-report-app-store-us-157894 https://nutrola.app/en/blog/what-do-reddit-users-say-about-foodvisor-2026 https://unitq.com/unitq-scorecards/yuka
- Lesson for Pro EUR 8/mo: keep all core visualisation free; never paywall something previously free; make cancellation one click; no login required (privacy as differentiator, "everything runs in your browser").

## 5. Caffeine / alcohol calculators
- Caffinity, LastSip, Caffeine Health (open-source, offline, F-Droid; age/weight/smoking/CYP1A2 meds), Peerlist half-life calc (fixed 5 h). https://mwm.ai/apps/caffinity-caffeine-tracker/6770263224 https://mwm.ai/apps/lastsip-caffeine-coffee/6775479672 https://cloudflare.f-droid.org/fr/packages/com.uc.caffeine https://peerlist.io/tumbleweed/project/caffeine-half-life-calculator
- Do well: bedtime prediction, personal half-life (weight/smoking/meds). Weak: charts only, tracking-focused (manual logging chore).
- BAC calculators: Widmark limits (absorption, individual metabolism, 70/60 kg assumptions); Apple rejected a BAC app lacking hardware. https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/114265
- Legal/safety: Inside 24h driving answer must show ranges, say "never use to decide to drive", and avoid precise BAC number claims; keep A/B/C evidence labels.
- Do better: personal sliders (weight, sex, smoker, pill, pregnancy for caffeine half-life; food in stomach for alcohol) feeding the 3D animation and bedtime/driving answers.

## 6. 3D anatomy apps
- Complete Anatomy: ~$60 individual / $75 student / $100-120 pro per year, 3-day trial. Zygote Body: free Lite, Premium ~$4/mo (undated). BioDigital: free tier + paid. Common Sense: Zygote praised for saved annotated images, lacks sharing. https://intuitionlabs.ai/software/pdfs/complete-anatomy.pdf https://www.commonsense.org/education/reviews/zygote-body https://pricing.biodigital.com/
- They are static anatomy atlases for students: no time dimension, no food, no physiology simulation. Inside 24h = "dynamic physiology on anatomy". Copy: saved/shared views (deep-link URL to a food+camera state), organ labels with layer toggles, teacher/classroom mode.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills
1. Free, no-signup, any-food, 24 h, 3D organ-level timeline.
2. Evidence-graded claims (A/B/C/?) with sources on a consumer health visual - viral content has none.
3. Comparison of two foods with "better swap".
4. Output formatted for social (Reel 9:16/4:5) - drives organic growth.
5. Hydration by city / water quality combined with food effects.
6. Energy as minutes of walking, and driving/sleep quick answers in one place.
7. Educational/classroom use (schools lack drink-focused body tools; Strong Spirit is only alcohol).
