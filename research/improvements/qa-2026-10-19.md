## Open: product/science questions (not changed)
1. **Our kebab is lean against the same study:** Panozzo measured 226 kcal/100 g (901 kcal per 402 g); ours is 174 kcal/100 g (574 kcal per 330 g): chicken thigh, yogurt sauce, no dripping fat. Use a fattier döner meat record or more sauce? Needs a source.
2. **Pizza "Blood sugar: not measured".** Atkinson 2008 does list pizza GIs; the database has none, so pizza and every pizza meal show no curve. A dietitian will notice. Research item.
3. **The first visit is two full screens** (about you, then choose) before any body is seen. A TikTok visitor might prefer one tap on a tile first, body questions later.
