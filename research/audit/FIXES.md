# Food data fixes (2026-10-17)

Applied from the four audit reports (food-drinks, food-protein, food-produce, food-grains) after checking each one against the USDA SR Legacy CSV, Atkinson 2008 Supplementary Tables A1/A2 (re-downloaded from Europe PMC and re-read), or the product label. Backup: `backups/20261017-194549-fooddata/`. Data flow and mechanisms: `research/fooddb-notes.md` ("Data flow"). Tests: `app/src/v4/foodData.test.ts`.

Per-serving values = per 100 g × serving grams. "label" = override with its own source shown in the app ("How do we know?" and the How we know page).

## Labels (American English, metric)

US cup labels → "1 portion, X g" / "1 bowl, X g" (rice, legumes, vegetables, soups, yogurt, oatmeal, pasta "1 portion, 124 g cooked"); pork chop "1 chop"; chicken "½ breast"; salmon "½ fillet"; cottage cheese "1 portion, 105 g"; tofu, ice cream "1 portion"; cherries "1 bowl, 140 g"; popcorn "1 cup, 8 g"; plural counts ("2 slices"); names: "Pancakes (US-style)" (proxy for palačinke), "Bean soup (with ham)", "Black olives", "Spinach (cooked)", "Doughnut (jam-filled)", "Butter (unsalted)". Vegetable and chicken soup flagged proxy. Movie preset sub "air-popped, 3 cups" (no oil-popped popcorn in the DB).
